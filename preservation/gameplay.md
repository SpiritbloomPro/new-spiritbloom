Preservation is a somewhat complicated healer. It's heavily based around the usage of several short-cooldowns and the empowered spells, it lacks a real useful spammable healing button,
has a shorter range than any other class and it weirdly goes from having a lot of mobility tools to also being constantly immobile casting long channels.

Using just the right amount of resources to respond to any given situation without overcommiting cooldowns that you will need soon is a key aspect of succeeding as a preservation evoker.

## Echo
  
This is the key component of the spec. When you cast !Echo! on someone, the next healing spell you use **on any target** will be replicated on that person, consuming the
!Echo!. Covering your group with !Echo! and then casting a spell is how the spec uses it's resource to increase it's healing and to cover more targets than what the spells
are normally capable of. Good !Echo! management is key to playing preservation well.

For a detailed list on all the ways !Echo! interacts with the rest of our spells you can check [the interactions page on !Echo!](/preservation/interactions#echo)

## Empowered Spells
    
Preservation has two empowered spells: !Dream Breath! and !Fire Breath!

!Dream Breath! is a frontal cone aoe with both an upfront heal and a heal-over-time. The higher you empower it the more healing the initial hit does in exchange a shorter over-time heal. This is your healing empower spell and the centerpiece of most of your kit. When !Echo!ed, it will do it's upfront healing and apply a second version of its heal over time on any target that had an !Echo!.

The second empower, !Fire Breath!, is a damage spell and it works in the same way as !Dream Breath!. !Fire Breath! is still an important part of our healing kit because of !Life-Givers Flame! and !Leaping Flames!. Being a damage spell, !Fire Breath! can't be !Echo!ed.

Casting !Fire Breath! will give you the !Leaping Flames! buff, which makes your next !Living Flame! cast one extra flame per level you empowered the !Fire Breath!. These extra
!Living Flame!s can proc their own !Essence Burst!s and will be aimed at enemies if your !Living Flame! target was an enemy or at allies if you used it to heal. If you cast
!Living Flame! on an enemy with !Leaping Flames! up but theres not enough hostile units close enough to target with the !Living Flame!s any extras will heal nearby allies instead.

The other part is !Life-Givers Flame!, which excels in aoe scenarios. On mythic+ unleashing a max rank !Fire Breath! on at least five hostile targets it's enough to heal your whole party back up, making it just as good as our dedicated healing spells at healing your group.

## Merithra's Blessing

Our apex talent !Merithra's Blessing! has a chance to activate when you spend essence and also does so every time you use !Dream Breath!. It replaces your !Reversion! spell with a powered-up version that has a stronger initial wave of healing that then bounces around to different nearby injured targets. !Merithra's Blessing! interacts very well with !Echo!, as the spell is replicated completely and will do all of its bounces for every !Echo! that you consume with it.

Using !Merithra's Blessing! also carries with it all the normal effects of a !Reversion!, which include !Golden Hour! and applying the heal over time effect that applies !Grace Period!
    
## Temporal Anomaly
    
Another fundamental part of the preservation toolkit is !Temporal Anomaly!. This is the tool that lets you quickly cover people with !Echo!es. Notably these !Echo!es are weaker than ones applied by manually casting the !Echo! spell: they replicate spells at 30% effectiveness baseline and 45% with !Timelord!, as opposed to casted !Echo!es which do 70% baseline and 105% with !Timelord!.

The tradeoff here is that !Temporal Anomaly! lets you apply !Echo! in a very quick and efficient way, not using essence at all, but in turn do less healing. In raids you will combine the two types of !Echo! to cover as many people as possible before you use healing spells, while in mythic+ you might choose between one or the other depending on a lot of factors like how much time you have before needing to heal, how much available essence you have, or what spell you are casting to consume them.

All the interactions that work on !Echo! do the same thing to !Echo!es applied by !Resonating Sphere!, they simply do smaller numbers. The one exception to this is
!Grace Period! which will always increase healing taken by 10%.
    
## Reversion
    
!Reversion! is a spell that on its own is just a heal over time that gets extended on critical hits. The complexity of this button comes from all the extra effects it gets added to it via talents, like !Golden Hour!, !Lifespark! and !Grace Period!, on top of the resource generation it provides by having a chance to give us !Essence Burst! on application.

While you might not find yourself !Echo!ing the base version of !Reversion! a lot, you gain all of its benefits when you use !Merithra's Blessing! which means all the effects that increase its power are very valuable anyway. !Reversion! is also our best spot-healing tool as !Golden Hour! scales very well with high damage taken in a short period of time.

For more information on how !Reversion! interacts with the rest of our kit you can read the [interactions page on !Reversion!](/preservation/interactions#reversion)

## Emerald Blossom
    
!Emerald Blossom! is a very unique spell that serves as our alternative to !Echo! for using essence. To note !Emerald Blossom! usually costs a fair bit of mana, but the effect of !Essence Burst! not only makes spell cost no essence but also removes the mana cost, making blossoms much easier to fit into your gameplay.

Due to !Twin Echoes!, !Emerald Blossom! represents a key part of our gameplay as you constantly weave between your essence consumption tools to maximize the value you get. Using your !Essence Burst!s in !Emerald Blossom! will do a good amount of healing and also give you a stack of !Twin Echoes!, which you can then use with your natural essence (as !Echo! is very cheap mana wise) to apply just as many !Echo!es as you would have if you consumed the !Essence Burst! with it.


!Emerald Blossom! also comes with a whole set of interactions and talent support like !Fluttering Seedlings!, !Field of Dreams!, and Flameshaper's !Consume Flame!.
