Preservation has been historically a ramp healer, which means its gameplay is divided between setting up !Echo!es across the raid followed by consuming them for high burst damage at a specific timing. This identity has been diminished lately as these days Preservation plays in a much more fluid and dynamic way: While !Echo! is still a central component of the spec, you constantly go from applying it to people to consuming it with a spell in short bursts rather than large coverage.

The result is that you still follow mostly the same principles as before, but now you go from !Echo! spreading into consumptions much more often and repeatedly, resulting in a more consistent healing output that also requires properly reacting to procs.

## The Source Of Your Healing

As !Echo! is still the centerpiece of the spec the majority of your healing will still come from properly utilizing it to replicate spells across the raid. Your strongest option to consume !Echo!es with is !Merithra's Blessing!. The combination of the high upfront heal from !Merithra's Blessing! and !Golden Hour! plus the heal over time of !Reversion! and the increase via !Grace Period! make this ability the ideal choice for every time you need to consume !Echo!es.

New to Midnight thanks to !Twin Echoes!, !Emerald Blossom! now also represents an important part of our healing. Going into season two with the swap into Flameshaper which brings !Consume Flame!, the importance of properly utilizing blossoms has risen, making it another of the key points to getting as much healing as you can out of your character.

These two points combined mean that the central thing to focus on is **proper essence management**. Utilizing all your !Essence Burst!s for !Emerald Blossom!s to avoid the high mana cost, while maximizing the value of !Twin Echoes! and your natural essence to apply as many !Echo!es as possible before consuming them with !Merithra's Blessing! will be your focus.

## Important Things to Know

- !Temporal Anomaly! and !Dream Breath! are very potent tools at your disposal that you should use pretty much on cooldown. !Temporal Anomaly! enables most of your !Echo! spread and gives you more casts of !Dream Breath! via !Nozdormus Teachings!, while !Dream Breath! does a lot of healing by itself, gives you casts of !Merithra's Blessing!, and is needed for !Consume Flame! to work.
- !Verdant Embrace! has fallen off a bit in the latest seasons. It is still an ok tool for spot healing but it can be very dangerous to use as most raid builds won't run !Dream Simulacrum!. It should still be used often but mostly on yourself as an !Essence Burst! generator via our tierset 4-piece bonus.
- It can be important that you target different players that are hurt with each of your !Emerald Blossom! casts. While before this didn't matter as the blossom simply picked targets inside its area, our tierset 2-piece bonus will cast free !Living Flame!s to your target when spending essence which means using it on someone that doesn't need that much healing at the moment makes you lose some value.

## Gameplay

You will use !Temporal Anomaly! and !Dream Breath! pretty much on cooldown, use all !Essence Burst!s you generate to cast !Emerald Blossom! and the !Twin Echoes! procs you get from that to apply !Echo! to the raid. You will then consume these !Echo!es with !Merithra's Blessing! and keep repeating the cycle depending on what resources you have available.

1. Use !Temporal Anomaly! on cooldown, this will apply !Echo! and shields to your raid as it passes through them, and also reduce the cooldown of !Dream Breath! and !Fire Breath! by 5 seconds each time due to !Nozdormus Teachings!.
2. Use !Dream Breath! on cooldown but avoid consuming !Echo!es with it. You have two charges of this spell so you can bank the second one for specific situations where you need more healing. Using !Dream Breath! gives you a !Merithra's Blessing! proc so you should use it before spreading !Echo!es to guarantee you can consume them with your apex.
3. Use all your !Essence Burst!s on !Emerald Blossom!. Blossoms are very expensive mana wise but !Essence Burst! makes them free, so you want to maximize this in combination with !Twin Echoes!. There can be some nuance in how to choose between a blossom and an echo depending on specific circumstances and resources but the general rule of thumb that you can't break is to always use !Emerald Blossom! with !Essence Burst!s and never with natural essence.
4. Use all your essence to apply !Echo!es, this will consume your !Twin Echoes! stacks to apply several !Echo!es at once every cast
5. Consume your !Echo!es with !Merithra's Blessing!.
6. Use !Fire Breath!. The !Leaping Flames! buff you will get is another relevant source of healing and can each !Living Flame! can generate !Essence Burst!.
6. The rest of the time, cast !Living Flame! on an enemy.

[-Rewind-]

!Rewind! is a press-and-forget raid healing cooldown that simply restores health equal to 30% of the damage your raid members have taken in the last 5 seconds. The ideal usage is right after a big wave of burst damage has hit the raid. Note that the healing from !Rewind! comes in the form of a 5-second heal over time, that will do all the corresponding healing spread across these 5 seconds.

Taking more damage while the !Rewind! hot is active won't increase its healing, the amount is decided at the moment you press the spell and it is simply delayed over the following seconds.

[-Dreamflight-]

!Dreamflight! is a raid cooldown that does an inital wave of healing to everyone you fly over and applies a strong heal over time effect to them. The initial wave of healing that happens as you fly is an important part of the power of the spell so you need to use it *after damage has already happened* and not before.

One of the drawbacks of the spell is that it requires you to fly across your raid with no control of your movement so a miscalculation can make you go face first into dangerous boss mechanics. To compensate for this while dreamflying you will get a shield equal to your maximum hp, but you can still be in danger as the shield will drop off as soon as you land which might still be in a dangerous position

## Conclusions

Preservation currently lives in a much more simpler and approachable format than what it has historically had, there is less complexity in the way you have to manage ramps and timings and you can mostly react to your resources and cooldowns as they happen in the moment. This doesn't mean that the spec is easy by any means and you still need to keep your focus and remember how to handle different scenarios with a very specialized kit.

One thing Preservation does maintain is the deeply rewarding feeling of mastering its mechanics and executing properly. A well player Preservation Evoker is one of the most fun and interactive things you can do in this game, and that has remained true over the years. This will still be the case for Midnight Season 2, where Preservation looks to be in a very good spot power wise.
