### Class Tree

- A lot of the points are utility nodes and can be moved around as needed, like !Earthgrab Totem!, !Improved Purify Spirit!, !Winds of Alakir!, and !Wind Shear!.
- !Earth Elemental! and !Primordial Bond! are an option if you need an extra defensive.
- !Thunderous Paws! is an option if you need to clear snares, !Spirit Wolf! is generally stronger as a defensive.

### Spec Tree

- !Ancestral Reach! and !Flow of the Tides! are both good but !Ancestral Reach! is easier to get value of during raids and requires less focus. On paper !Flow of the Tides! can lead to more healing but it requires you to play into it a lot more.
- !Overflowing Shores! is a very good increase in quality of life but not a very high healing increase, its main value is in a good pathing option to !Rip Current!. The only real place to move the point into would be !Current Control! but the mana reduction in !Healing Wave! is fairly useless.
- !Earthen Accord! is a very low value point, it doesn't increase your healing by a good amount and its not worth considering.

[-Rotation-]

### Normal Gameplay

1. Use all !Stormstream Totem! procs you get
2. Keep !Riptide! on cooldown. Prefer targets that don't already have it
3. Use !Natures Swiftness! on cooldown
4. Maintain !Surging Totem! active
5. Use !Downpour! as much as possible
6. Use !Healing Stream Totem!. Keep it on cooldown as much as possible
7. Cast !Unleash Life!. It doesn't matter too much what spell you consume the buff with
8. Fill with !Chain Heal! or !Healing Wave!. !Chain Heal! does much more overall healing but costs more mana, !Healing Wave! is cheaper and also better at healing one or two targets.

### Ascendance

1. Cast !Riptide! twice to get two !Tidal Waves! stacks
2. Use !Unleash Life! to activate the buff
3. Press !Ascendance!
4. Cast !Chain Heal! twice
5. Use !Natures Swiftness!
6. Cast !Chain Heal!
7. Use all your !Healing Stream Totem!s and !Stormstream Totem!s
8. Cast !Chain Heal! while !Ascendance! is active

[-Stats-]

Your best stat is Critical Strike and you want as much of it as possible. After that Versatility and Mastery are fairly close in value while Haste is your worse stat.

1. Critical Strike
2. Versatility = Mastery
3. Haste

[-EndTabs-]

For more details on how to optimize your gameplay for raids, check the [raids page of the guide](/restoration-shaman/raid.md).

### Class Tree

- A lot of the points are utility nodes and can be moved around as needed, like !Earthgrab Totem!, !Improved Purify Spirit!, and !Purge!. !Poison Cleansing Totem! and !Tremor Totem! can be good pickups depending on the dungeon.
- !Earth Elemental! and !Primordial Bond! are an option if you need an extra defensive.
- !Thunderous Paws! is an option if you need to clear snares, !Spirit Wolf! is generally stronger as a defensive.

### Spec Tree

- !Ancestral Reach! and !Flow of the Tides! are both good, !Flow of the Tides! is stronger on paper but requires you to properly play into it.
- !Ancestral Vigor! is a very good utility option but costly at 2 points. If you are not doing dungeons where the extra health is needed you can move these points into other options like !Overflowing Shores! and !Rip Current!.
- !Crashing Waves! is good but its costly as it requires you to also pick !Current Control!.
- !Deeply Rooted Elements! is very strong but can be a bit too random. The point can also be moved into !Downpour! for a more controlled form of healing. !Earthen Harmony! can then be moved into !Water Expulsion!.
