### Class Tree

- A lot of the points are utility nodes and can be moved around as needed, like !Earthgrab Totem!, !Improved Purify Spirit!, !Winds of Alakir!, and !Wind Shear!.
- !Earth Elemental! and !Primordial Bond! are an option if you need an extra defensive.
- !Thunderous Paws! is an option if you need to clear snares, !Spirit Wolf! is a more straight forward defensive.

### Spec Tree

- !Overflowing Shores! is the lowest value point in the tree and can be moved around. Some options are !Improved Earthliving Weapon!, !Unleash Life!, and !Crashing Waves!.
- !Downpour! can be very good but also very annoying to play and costly in talent points.
- Consider !First Ascendant! in raid bosses that work on two-minute timers.

[-Rotation-]

### Normal Gameplay

1. Use all !Stormstream Totem! procs you get
2. Keep !Riptide! on cooldown. Prefer targets that don't already have it
3. Use !Natures Swiftness! on cooldown
4. Maintain !Surging Totem! active
6. Use !Healing Stream Totem!. Keep it on cooldown as much as possible
8. Fill with !Chain Heal! or !Healing Wave!. !Chain Heal! does much more overall healing but costs more mana, !Healing Wave! is cheaper and also better at healing one or two targets.

### Ascendance

To maximize the power of !Ascendance!, follow this priority:

1. Cast !Riptide! twice to get two !Tidal Waves! stacks
2. Press !Ascendance!
3. Use !Natures Swiftness!
4. Use all your !Healing Stream Totem!s and !Stormstream Totem!s
5. Cast !Chain Heal! while !Ascendance! is active

[-Stats-]

Your best stat is Critical Strike and you want as much of it as possibly, after that Haste is your strongest second option followed by Versatility and finally Mastery.

1. Critical Strike
2. Haste
3. Versatility
4. Mastery

### Class Tree

- A lot of the points are utility nodes and can be moved around as needed, like !Earthgrab Totem!, !Improved Purify Spirit!, and !Purge!. !Poison Cleansing Totem! and !Tremor Totem! can be good pickups depending on the dungeon.
- !Earth Elemental! and !Primordial Bond! are an option if you need an extra defensive.
- !Thunderous Paws! is an option if you need to clear snares, !Spirit Wolf! is generally stronger as a defensive.
- !Spirit Walk! can remove a lot of very dangerous roots present in dungeons.

### Spec Tree

- !Healing Tide Totem! is a good option in keys where you can't always guarantee being able to stand still to cast during !Ascendance! and you can't always commit !Spitiwalkers Grace! along with it.
- !First Ascendant! is a better pair to !Healing Tide Totem! than !Preeminence! but it does make your !Deeply Rooted Elements! procs worse.
- !Ancestral Vigor! can be very good but its value its hard to quantify, if you find yourself you don't need the extra health you can move the points into healing increases like !Wavespeakers Blessing!, !Overflowing Shores!, or !Unleash Life!.
