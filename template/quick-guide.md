- A lot of the points are utility nodes and can be moved around as needed, like Spell One, Spell Two, or Spell Three.

### Spec Tree

- Don't change these talents because they are important even though some idiots don't grab them.
- You can change IrrelevantChoiceNodeOne for IrrelevantChoiceNodeTwo if you want.
- ComplexTalentOne can be an hps increase but its harder to use.

### Normal Gameplay

1. Use the good cooldowns first
2. Don't forget to use the efficient spells as well
3. Using the good cooldowns procs the good spells, consume those
4. Fill with the crappy spells after
