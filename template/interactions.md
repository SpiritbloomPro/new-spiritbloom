Here you will find details on how this specs spells work. The goal here is to document in depth how the spells actually interact with each other and with other mechanics in order to understand them better.

## Specialization Spells

### Spell One

This spell has a series of unintuitive shits. For example:

- Issue number one is a pain in the ass.
- Issue number two only happens during full moons.
- Issue number three only heals your godparents and not your friends.

### Spell Two

This spell has an incredibly crappy tooltip so im gonna tell you how it really works. First it does this, then it does that, then it makes you do a handstand, and *then* only then it actually heals people. You should be aware of this bullshit during combat.
