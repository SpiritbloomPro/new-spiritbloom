# Midnight S1 M+ Utility for Preservation Evoker

This list is curated by Cro. If anything is missing or incorrect, do not hesitate to ping @Cronox in the [Spiritbloom Discord server](http://discord.spiritbloom.pro/). Much of the utility below is mainly related to Pres Evoker, but any bleed dispel, soothe and snare removal respectively will work in most cases.

## Enrages (Soothable)

This season has some enrages where Oppressing Roar with Overawe talented is high value - for example the eagles in AA and Mystic in WS, where AOE-soothing enemies in a cone in front of you will significantly decrease the damage dealt by the packs.

This season has plenty of bleeds. While some of them are single-target and not too frequent, others - like Crawth in AA or Ranjit in SKY - may be worth committing Stasis to, [as listed here](https://spiritbloom.pro/preservation/interactions#stasis).

## Root / Snare / Grip removal (Rescue)

There are some situations where Rescue can be very useful. Some abilities have damaging components tied to roots, snares or grips, and a well-used Rescue can clear these effects from both the rescuing Evoker and the person being rescued.

### AA (Algeth'ar Academy)

No roots/snares/grips found.

Unfortunately Rescue will not be back off CD before the 2nd time. Rescuing the sacrificed person down from the altar is amazing and will earn you a special place in that person's heart. |
